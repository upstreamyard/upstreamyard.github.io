3. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels or fingerprinting and no social media plugins, and it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other servers.

The only information stored on your device is one entry in your browser's local storage, so that the cookie notice is shown only on your first visit:

Name: upstreamyard-cookie-notice
Content: the date the notice was shown, nothing else
Purpose: not showing the cookie notice again
Storage period: until you clear your browser data
Shared with: no one; it never leaves your browser

This entry is strictly necessary to provide the notice as intended, so it does not require consent (article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002, LSSI-CE). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings; the notice will then appear once more. The "Copy" buttons only write to your clipboard when you click them; nothing is saved.

If this ever changes, we will update this section first and ask for your consent wherever the law requires it.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (article 15)

To exercise these rights, email contact@upstreamyard.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work. Our supervisory authority is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).
